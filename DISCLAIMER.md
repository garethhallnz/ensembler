# Disclaimer

**Ensembler is a configuration tool.** It installs, starts, and connects
third-party self-hosted applications (such as Sonarr, Radarr, Plex, Jellyfin,
Prowlarr, and Transmission) on your own computer, so you don't have to
configure them by hand.

## Content neutrality

- Ensembler ships with **no media content and no content sources**. It contains
  no indexers, no trackers, and no lists or presets of either, and it will
  never add, suggest, or link to any.
- Ensembler **never selects, requests, searches for, or downloads content**.
  Any content that flows through the services you run is the result of sources
  *you* choose and actions *you* take.
- Configuring an indexer or tracker is a step Ensembler deliberately leaves to
  you. The project does not provide guidance on finding or choosing content
  sources, and requests for such guidance (in issues, discussions, or pull
  requests) will be declined.
